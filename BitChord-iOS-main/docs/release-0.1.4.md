## What's new in 0.1.4

### Added
- **Liquid Glass mini player.** On iOS 26 the mini player is part of the tab bar and uses Liquid Glass.
- **Mini player folds into the tab bar.** Scroll down and the tab bar shrinks, with the mini player between the tab buttons and Search as its own button, like Apple Music. Scroll up to bring it back.
- **Liquid Glass sheets.** Audio output, Audio pipeline, the song menu, Switch account, the lyrics sheets and the Listen Together members sheet now use Liquid Glass on iOS 26.

### Changed
- Removed the blank space under the last row of Settings, Library, History and artist pages. The version line now sits right above the mini player.
- Fixed the solid strip behind the mini player and tab bar on the On Device folders (Downloads and Local Music). The lists now scroll behind the glass.
- On iOS 17 to 25 the mini player and sheets look the same as before.
