## What's new in 0.1.3

### Added
- **BitChord-iOS finally hit the files app.** BitChord now has its own folder in the Files app: Files > On My iPhone > BitChord.
- **Music folder.** Downloaded songs are saved in BitChord > Music. Songs you downloaded before this version move there on their own the first time you open the app.
- **AnimatedArt folder.** Animated artwork is saved in BitChord > AnimatedArt, with a folder for each album, and still plays offline.
- **Diagnostics folder.** The diagnostic log is saved in BitChord > Diagnostics. It is written while "Keep diagnostic log" is on in Settings.

### Changed
- Animated artwork is no longer cleared when BitChord updates.
- Animated artwork saved by 0.1.2 is not carried over. It downloads again the next time its song plays.
